Connect your database to continue

The database is not connected. Your invoices, customers, items and settings are kept in CSV files on this computer. Until they are connected, nothing can be saved, so the app is locked.

  1. Click Connect Database.
  2. In the folder window, choose your data folder. A new, empty folder is fine: the app creates its data files there.
  3. Allow the browser to edit the files when it asks.
This browser cannot open the CSV folder. Open the app in the latest Chrome or Edge.
