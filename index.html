Get started in 4 steps

It takes about two minutes. You only do this once.

  • 1

    Install the app (optional)

    Press “Install app” above and confirm when the browser asks. If you do not want to install it, go to the next step and open the app in the browser.

  • 2

    Create your data folder

    On your computer, create a new empty folder anywhere, for example Documents\InvoiceGen-data. All your customers, items and invoices will be saved in this folder.

  • 3

    Connect the folder to the app

    Open the app and press Connect Database. Choose your folder and allow access when the browser asks. An empty folder is fine: the app creates its own files in it.

  • 4

    Add your business details

    Open Business & Invoice Setup and fill in Seller Info, Bank & UPI and Terms. Then add your customers and items and create your first invoice.

How to use it

  1. 1Download the file and unzip it. You get a folder called InvoiceGen-sample-data.
  2. 2Open the app and press Connect Database. Choose that unzipped folder.
  3. 3Look through Business & Invoice Setup, Customer Details and Item Details to see how the entries look.

Do not use the sample folder for your real business. The invoices you try out would stay in it.

Good to know

Where is my data stored?

Your data stays on your computer. Nothing is uploaded to the internet.

Does it work without internet?

After the first visit the app works without internet. A new version arrives by itself when you are online.

Which browsers and devices work?

It works only in Chrome or Edge, because they can read and write a folder on your computer. It does not work on phones.

How do I back up my data?

Your folder is the only copy of your data. Back it up regularly. The app also has Backup & Restore.

The browser asks for permission again

If the browser asks for permission again, press Connect Database and choose the same folder. Your data is safe.

Can I use it on another computer?

The installed app and the browser version can use the same folder. To use it on another computer, install the app there and copy your folder across.
